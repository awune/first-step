Before you start

This is a small test of a tool that turns what's on your mind into one tiny first step. It is a productivity and wellbeing support tool. It is not therapy, medical advice or an emergency service.

What happens to what you say
  • If you use the mic, your phone's own speech service (Apple or Google) turns your voice into text. That part is theirs, under their rules.
  • The text is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to write your step. We do not save it. Under its business terms, Anthropic deletes it within 30 days and does not use it to train its models.
What we save
  • Your pilot code (not your name), the step you were given, the times, whether you started, and your rating.
  • No audio. No location. No contacts.
Your choices
  • Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time.
  • To have your data deleted, message the person who sent you this link and give them your code.
  • You need to be 18 or over.

If you are struggling, you can call Samaritans free on 116 123 (UK, any time). In an emergency call 999, or your local emergency number.
